## MOC v4.6 — Marketing Operations Catalyst

The primary diagnostic engine. Governs how operational signals are captured, classified, and routed into intelligence outputs.

**Key layers active in this repo:**
- **ASIL** (Step 0A.5) — Ambient Signal Intelligence Layer: captures signals before structured diagnostic begins
- **DIL** (Step 1.5) — signal-to-intelligence translation
- **PIL** (Step 6.5) — assumption validity check mid-execution (Monitor / Prepare / Act)
- **OAL** (Step 8.2) — single-threaded ownership enforcement before output ships
- **BIL v1.0** (Step 5.25) — PHANTOM SIGNAL gate (blocks false positives)
- **AIL v1.0** — INSIGHT THEATER gate (blocks output that looks like insight but isn't)

**Scoring outputs MOC produces:**
OFI · SLR · DLS · AITG · ACR · AIS · ENS · CGM · DBL · BIL · DRL · AIL · PIL · OAL

## ESIL v1.0 — External Signal Intelligence Layer

Governs what this repo is and what it is not. ESIL constrains all artifacts to Layer 1 (externally observable signals only).

**ESIL enforces:**
- No internal data claims without a live engagement
- No speculative capability as deployed reality
- All hypotheses labeled as hypotheses, not findings
- Classification badge on every artifact: CONDITIONAL_PURSUE or equivalent

**ESIL signal categories used in tcp-commerce-discovery:**
- Marketplace Exposure signals
- Competitive Pressure signals
- Dormant Data Asset signals

## CES — Criteria Evaluation System

Scores AI leverage hypotheses across:
- Value potential
- Implementation feasibility
- Adoption resistance
- Governance risk
- Quick-win sequencing priority

Used in Sprint 2 (Opportunity Prioritization) — not exposed in Layer 1 agents.

---

## HCDG — Human-Centered Decision Governance

Fires before any AI recommendation is surfaced to a client or decision-maker.

**HCDG classification outputs:**
- PASS — AI belongs in this workflow
- CONDITIONAL — AI belongs with human checkpoint
- FAIL — AI Theater risk; do not proceed

Every agent in this repo that surfaces recommendations must be HCDG-aware. Agent outputs are intelligence for human decision, not autonomous actions.
